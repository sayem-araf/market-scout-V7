Assumptions & boundaries
    Historical sales are a simple demand proxy. Reservations are treated as separately committed stock; the projected baseline represents additional demand. There is no seasonality, service-level optimisation or forecast-accuracy claim. All lead times are calendar days. Prices exclude recoverable VAT on costs; VAT is a scenario input.
    Quotation ranking checks cost, timing, budget and margin only. Product quality, compliance, supplier capacity and commercial terms still require verification. Budgets apply per product, not to the whole purchasing portfolio.
